About Hessab Pro

Empowering businesses with innovative POS and management solutions

Our Story

Hessab Pro was born from a simple observation: small and medium-sized businesses needed powerful, affordable, and easy-to-use point-of-sale and business management software. In 2020, our founders, experienced entrepreneurs themselves, set out to create a solution that would level the playing field.

Today, Hessab Pro serves over 10,000 businesses across India, from small retail shops to large multi-location enterprises. Our mission remains unchanged: to provide world-class business management tools that are accessible to everyone.

Our Mission

To empower businesses of all sizes with innovative technology that simplifies operations, increases efficiency, and drives growth.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted and widely-used business management platform, helping millions of businesses thrive in the digital age.
